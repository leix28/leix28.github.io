### About Me

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at [Thinking Machines Lab](https://thinkingmachines.ai) working on pretraining.

Previously, I was an ML scientist at [OpenEvidence](https://www.openevidence.com), where I worked on AI systems for medical questions-answering and clinical decision support, and an Applied Scientist at [Amazon AWS](https://www.amazon.science/author/lei-xu), where I worked on LLMs for medical scribing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [MIT](http://www.mit.edu) in 2023, researching language model robustness and synthetic data, and my B.Eng. from [Tsinghua University](http://www.tsinghua.edu.cn).
